Compare the sound of violins

Open WAV recordings of bowed glissandi, scales or musical passages, or of bridge taps, to see their long-time-average spectra, band levels, note levels and tap-tone damping. Repeated takes are grouped automatically from the file names, so you can compare instruments or before and after an adjustment.
